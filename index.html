Parent & Teacher Liaison Support

Where appropriate, we work closely with parents and teachers to better understand each learner's strengths, challenges, and academic expectations.

  • Communication with teachers regarding academic concerns
  • Monitoring learner progress and feedback
  • Identifying areas requiring additional support
  • Assisting parents in understanding school expectations
  • Creating a unified support structure between home, school, and tutor

The TTC Partnership Promise

Meaningful academic growth happens when learners are supported by a strong, connected team.

Our commitment is to keep parents informed, learners supported, and educators part of the conversation whenever additional academic guidance is required.

By bridging the gap between home, school, and tuition, TTC creates an environment where students can thrive, develop confidence, and reach their full potential.
